World Tiger Population Visualization

A data visualization project exploring historical tiger population trends, geographical distribution, and conservation progress around the world.
Tigers are among the world's most threatened large carnivores. Understanding their population trends is essential for evaluating conservation progress and identifying areas that require further attention. By transforming raw population data into visual representations, this project aims to make wildlife conservation information easier to explore, understand, and communicate.

- Project Overview:
Wild tigers have experienced significant population declines over the past century due to habitat loss, poaching, and other human-related pressures. However, conservation efforts in several countries have contributed to population recovery.
This project visualizes tiger population data to make these trends easier to understand through charts and graphical comparisons.

- The visualizations focus on five key areas:
Global tiger population trends throughout history.
Latest estimated tiger populations by country.
Annual tiger mortality in India.
Tiger population growth across Nepal's national parks.
Tiger population distribution across Indian states.

- Dataset Source: Kaggle:Dataset: World Wild Tiger Population by Country , 1900–2026 by Robin Singh

This project uses five datasets extracted from the original dataset archive.

1. Global Tiger Population (1900–2023)

Dataset: global_tiger_population.csv

A line chart illustrating changes in the estimated global wild tiger population from 1900 to 2023.

This visualization highlights the dramatic decline in tiger populations over the decades, followed by signs of recovery in more recent years.

2. Latest Tiger Population by Country

Dataset: latest_estimate_by_country.csv

A horizontal bar chart comparing the latest estimated wild tiger populations across different countries.

India stands out as the country with the largest wild tiger population, with an estimated population of more than 3,600 tigers.

3. Tiger Mortality in India (2012–2023)

Dataset: india_tiger_mortality_by_year.csv

A line chart showing the number of recorded tiger deaths in India each year between 2012 and 2023, covering all recorded causes.

4. Tiger Population Across Nepal's National Parks

Dataset: nepal_tigers_by_national_park.csv

A multi-line chart illustrating tiger population changes across major protected areas in Nepal, including:

Chitwan National Park
Bardia National Park
Parsa National Park

The chart compares population trends across different national parks over their respective survey years.

5. Tiger Population by Indian State (2022)

Dataset: india_tigers_by_state.csv

A bar chart ranking Indian states according to their tiger populations in the 2022 national survey.

States such as Madhya Pradesh and Karnataka are among the major contributors to India's wild tiger population.

Project Objectives
Explore historical trends in global wild tiger populations.
Compare tiger populations across different countries.
Examine recorded tiger mortality trends in India.
Analyze tiger population growth in Nepal's national parks.
Identify Indian states with significant tiger populations.
Present wildlife conservation data in a clear and accessible visual format.
